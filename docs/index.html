The slip is the point

Job-work ERP targets large manufacturers; the small unit has a pile of chits. Nagdar accumulates the period across workers and rate-categories and produces a printable slip — a dispute-avoidance artifact for semi-literate piece workers.

Each line pays (qty − rejects) × rate.
